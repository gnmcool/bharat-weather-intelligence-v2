## 3. Unexpected findings / method issues (no treatment applied)

1. **Zero-width intervals at the boundary.** When a cell has 0 hits, POD = 0 and FAR = 1 in every resample, so the
   percentile interval is [0, 0] or [1, 1]. Example: GFS lead 4 at 115.6 mm, POD 0.00 [0.00, 0.00], FAR 1.00
   [1.00, 1.00].
   - This affects 876 single-model ratio values, mostly exploratory slices.
   - A zero-width interval here is a property of the bootstrap at a count boundary. **It is not certainty.**
   - Proposal for review: label such intervals "degenerate at boundary" in reporting, or replace them for these
     cases by a count-based interval (e.g. Wilson) in a later version. Not applied.
2. **IMD versus ERA5.** Against ERA5, 35.6 mm events number 820 over 36 points, with higher POD for ECMWF (0.43 at
   lead 1) than against IMD (0.27). The two references are kept separate and never combined. Because ERA5 comes from
   ECMWF's model family, an ECMWF–ERA5 match is expected and supports no model difference.
3. **Samples differ between single-model cells.** ECMWF has fewer eligible pairs (23,494–23,664 against 23,766)
   because of its archive start (B, February 2024) and the April 2026 outage (C). Model comparisons therefore use
   only the shared-data cells.
