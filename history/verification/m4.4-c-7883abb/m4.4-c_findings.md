## 2. Source-resolution limitation (a reporting note, not a measured forecast error)

At leads 5–6, GFS rainfall source representation changes. The effect on the verification metric and on the
model-agreement distribution is unresolved.

The distribution shifts visibly: the IMD k = 3 group at 35.6 mm falls from 38–86 cases at leads 1–4 to 9 and 3 at
leads 5–6. This does not mean improved or degraded forecast skill.

## 3. Unexpected findings

1. **Most reference events fall in k = 0.** Of the IMD ≥ 35.6 mm events, 57 % at lead 1 (438 of 765) and 65 % at
   lead 4 (493 of 764) occurred when none of the three models forecast ≥ 35.6 mm. This agrees with the frequency
   bias below 1 in M4.4-B.
2. **Full agreement is rare at these thresholds.** At 35.6 mm, three-model agreement occurs on 38–86 of about
   23,600 IMD cases at leads 1–4, below the 100-case floor. At 64.5 mm the k = 2 and k = 3 groups are also below
   the floor, and at 115.6 mm only k = 0 is shown.
3. **No degenerate intervals.** None occurred in M4.4-C; every computed group has at least 10 events and at least
   one non-event.
4. **Zero-width difference intervals in the published M4.4-B results.** 58 model-difference rows have a
   zero-width interval at 0. In 32 of them both models' values are on a boundary. In the other 26 both models hit
   exactly the same events, so the difference is 0 in every replicate. These rows are not labelled, because the
   label is defined for boundary values. They are listed here for review.

## 4. Implications and limitations for M4.4-D (not started)

- **Different definitions:** M4.4-C uses a same-day, same-threshold k of 3. CORE's rule is "k of 3 within one
  level, within ±1 day of the peak", applied to a best-match blend. M4.4-C therefore does not verify CORE's
  agreement rule or CORE's outputs.
- **No IMD reference for the prospective period:** IMD 2026 is not published. Prospective CORE rain items from
  28 Sep 2026 can only be judged against ERA5, labelled secondary, until IMD 2026 exists.
- **Event floors will take time to reach:** the IMD base rates here are about 3.2 % (≥ 35.6 mm), 1.1 %
  (≥ 64.5 mm) and 0.3 % (≥ 115.6 mm) of point-days over 23 months, concentrated in the monsoon. With 36 points, the
  VM §11 floor of ≥ 20 events per level and the minimum of 90 prospective days mean Severe-level results are
  unlikely before the 2027 monsoon.
- **Source resolution in the prospective data:** the prospective archive's GFS forecasts come from the same source.
  The lead-5+ source-representation change will affect CORE items that depend on GFS at those leads. Its effect
  stays unresolved and should be carried into any M4.4-D reporting.
- **Findings 1 and 2 are measurements on raw models, not on CORE.** They suggest that multi-model agreement at
  these thresholds is uncommon. Whether that affects CORE's issued items can only be measured in M4.4-D, on CORE's
  own outputs.
